# Joyride platform kickstart (historical)

The [original engineering handoff](https://gitlab.joyfullee.me/nest/config/-/blob/ddefe6a8e0aecc42caac28d0acb3b12cd8ba96eb/docs/joyride-platform-kickstart-handoff.md)
records the original discovery, frontend collaboration, private asset intake,
and TEST preparation. Its pending-work lists and dormant-production statements
are historical. It is not authority to reopen completed work or replay approvals.

Joyride source is maintained in [joy/joyride](https://gitlab.joyfullee.me/joy/joyride).
Joy and Luna own frontend, branding, content and business policy; Talon owns
backend, security, delivery and infrastructure.

- [Release and environment boundaries](joyride-environment-boundaries.md) cover
  the separate live TEST and production services and deliberate promotion of a
  verified TEST artifact. Promotion does not authorize changes to content,
  production data, payments, member communications, or public launch.
- [Private TEST deployment](joyride-test-deployment.md) and [CI](joyride-ci.md)
  describe the app delivery path. [The v1 acceptance record](joyride-v1-acceptance.md)
  preserves the approved scope, including photos-only v1, and the acceptance evidence.
- The [informational site](joyride-informational-publication.md) remains retired;
  do not revive its separate publisher or deployment.
