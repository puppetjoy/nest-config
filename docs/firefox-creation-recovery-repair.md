# Firefox creation receipt and finite recovery repair (historical)

The [original incident and qualification record](https://gitlab.joyfullee.me/nest/config/-/blob/ab4ac81d9c2c6291e14de1815e83e464efc3db70/docs/firefox-creation-recovery-repair.md)
preserves the first repair, the disposable tests, and the original activation
conditions. Its candidate pin, pending status, and build and activation steps
are not instructions for the current serving Firefox revision.

Use the [current lifecycle and recovery guidance](firefox-reusable-browser-lifecycle.md#conventional-lifecycle).
A durable, correlated receipt can establish ownership only in its own native
generation. Preserve the sole observer for an unresolved creation. Do not adopt
windows by tree difference, URL, or title, and do not replay an uncertain action
or erase its journal. A newer Ready image does not establish the outcome of a
historical creation or authorize a restart or generation retirement.
